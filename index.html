What We Do

Everything behind your numbers, handled

One team for the day-to-day bookkeeping, the tax filings and the big-picture advice — so nothing falls between your bookkeeper, your tax agent and your accountant.

Bookkeeping & Month-End Close

Transactions categorised, accounts reconciled, journals and accruals posted on a fixed calendar. Your books close on time, every month.

Financial Statements

Profit & loss, balance sheet and cash flow prepared to standard — with plain-English commentary on what actually moved and why.

Tax Preparation & Planning

Corporate and personal returns prepared, reviewed and filed on time — plus planning through the year so the bill is never a surprise.

Payroll & Compliance

Payroll processed, statutory deductions handled and filing deadlines tracked — with a clear record behind every submission.

CFO & Growth Advisory

Budgets, forecasts, pricing and cash runway. Senior financial thinking on tap, without the cost of a full-time hire.

Systems & AI Automation

The right accounting stack, set up properly and connected — with AI handling the repetitive capture and matching, so data flows in cleanly instead of being retyped every month.
